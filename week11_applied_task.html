This Week's Task
Name the thing you've been holding. Then say it.

There's usually something. A concern that hasn't been raised. A peer dynamic that hasn't been addressed. Something you noticed that you didn't flag. Write it down here, decide who it goes to, and say it this week. Then come back and write what happened. That's what you'll share at the next meeting.

What is the thing you've been holding?

Be specific. Not "I sometimes feel like communication could be better." The actual thing — what you noticed, what you've been hesitant to raise, what keeps coming to mind when you think about what you haven't said yet.

Which direction does it go?

↑ Upward — to leadership
→ Lateral — to a peer
↻ Sideways — something I noticed

What has been stopping you from saying it?

Honestly. Not sure it's your place? Worried about how it lands? Waiting for the right moment? Hoping it resolves itself?

After you've said it — what happened?

Did you say it? How did you say it? How did the other person respond? Was it as hard as you thought it would be? What would you do differently? If the moment hasn't come yet, write what you're planning to say and update this after.
