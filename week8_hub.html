The Murphy Concept
Murphy is a donor dog whose owner will tell a hesitant parent "yeah, we've been doing this for two years — it's totally fine."

Murphy exists at every location that has existing donors. He's the most credible closer in the lobby — not because he says anything different from what you say, but because he's not you. He's a peer. Biscuit is a Murphy. The question this week is: who's yours?
