Part 1 — Name One Parent Per Location

For each of your active locations, name one parent — their name, their dog's name, and what your relationship with them has produced (a referral, a sign-up, an introduction, a conversation that wouldn't have happened without the relationship). If you can't name one at a location, write that honestly — that's what you're working on this week at that location.

One specific ask you made this week

At one of your locations, make a specific ask of a staff member that could turn them into someone who works for the program between your visits. "Would you be willing to text me when a new large dog joins the daycare?" Name the location, the staff member, and what you asked.

Part 2 — The Polite Brush-Off Location

Name one location that has been giving you vague, non-committal responses across multiple visits. How many times have you been there? What have you tried? Then pick your decision.
