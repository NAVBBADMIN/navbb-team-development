This Week — Two Parts
Your parent. Your closed door.

Part one: at each of your active locations, name one parent — not a manager, a parent — who you have a real relationship with. If you can't name one at a location, that's your work for this week. Part two: name one location that has been giving you the polite brush-off. Write what you've tried, how many times, and what your plan is going forward.

A parent whose dog is in the program or could be, who knows your name, who you have direct contact with. If you can't name one, write "building" and describe what your plan is for developing that relationship this week.
