A Patricia Moment
Patricia scored herself a 4 on mission clarity.

Patricia believes she is deeply connected to the mission. Patricia added four locations to her schedule this week because her numbers were down. Patricia has not thought about why the program matters in approximately six weeks. Patricia explains the program clearly and accurately. Patricia has not made anyone feel it in longer than she can remember. Patricia considers herself mission-driven.

Question 1 of 6 · Mission Clarity
I can name, right now, a specific reason the program matters — not the program description, but a real outcome for a real dog or family.
Not "it saves lives" as a phrase. A specific picture of what that means.
Question 2 of 6 · Mission Clarity
When I talk to a hesitant dog owner, I can feel the urgency of what I'm saying — not just recite it.
The difference between explaining the program and actually believing it in the moment.
Question 3 of 6 · Strategic vs. Reactive
When my numbers are slow, my first instinct is to ask what I should do differently — not just do more of the same things faster.
The difference between diagnosing the problem and increasing the volume.
Question 4 of 6 · Strategic vs. Reactive
When a partner location asks me to change something — a schedule, a visit day, a process — I ask whether the change serves the program before I agree.
Not whether it's convenient. Whether it serves the mission.
Question 5 of 6 · Resilience
When I get a rejection or a cold maybe, I release it faster because the mission continues regardless of that one conversation.
The rejection doesn't mean the work is failing. It means one conversation didn't land. The mission is bigger than any one conversation.
Question 6 of 6 · Resilience
I have a way of reconnecting to why the program matters when I feel myself drifting into just going through the motions.
A specific practice, memory, or habit — not just the intention to "remember why I do this."
