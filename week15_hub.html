The Pivot That Worked This Week
"We are making an investment in their health and want to make the greatest impact with their donations. We can't do that if they only donate once."
Said to a golden retriever owner who was already walking away. He stopped, asked for information, and gave his name for follow-up. One sentence changed the whole conversation.

What You Are Actually Asking For

A sign-up is a commitment to six donations over twelve months. Not a one-time thing. Not a try-it-and-see. The way you frame that commitment determines whether the parent who says yes is a donor or just a name on a list. How to have a conversation that earns the real yes.

Six scenes. An owner is already heading for the door. The standard pitch has not landed. One recruiter follows the script and loses them. The other pivots to the investment frame and gets the name. What made the difference and why it works when nothing else does.
