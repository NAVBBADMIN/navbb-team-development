A Patricia Moment
Patricia knows how many dogs she has signed up. Patricia does not know her show rate.

Patricia was asked her show rate this week. Patricia pulled up her sign-up count. Patricia was told that was not the same thing. Patricia then said her show rate is probably pretty good because she follows up with everyone. Patricia was asked how she follows up. Patricia said email. Patricia was asked what her email open rate was. Patricia did not know. Patricia is looking into a better email subject line.

Question 1 of 6 - The Call
I call pending donors two weeks before an event - not to remind them the date exists, but to confirm they are actually coming.
Two weeks out, by phone. Not email. Not a week out. Two weeks, so they still have time to plan and you still have time to do something about it if they are not coming.
Question 2 of 6 - The Call
When a donor does not answer, I leave a voicemail and call again later the same day - not days later, the same day.
Midday, then 2:30. Two calls on the same issue in the same day is not too much - it is evidence that you actually need them there.
Question 3 of 6 - Reading the Response
When I get a donor on the phone, I can tell the difference between a confident "yes we will be there" and a hesitant one - and I do something different with each.
A hesitant yes is a maybe in disguise. If you hear hesitation, dig into it. Do not just move on because they technically said yes.
Question 4 of 6 - The Archive Decision
After two calls and one email with no response, I archive the dog and note it as unresponsive. I do not keep carrying names that are not responding.
Not a punishment - a pipeline decision. Unresponsive names make your show rate look worse and take the same energy to manage as active donors who actually show up.
Question 5 of 6 - Pipeline Hygiene
I know roughly how many of my pending donors I have actually spoken to since their sign-up conversation.
Not emailed - spoken to. By phone, in person, or by text that generated a real back-and-forth response.
Question 6 of 6 - Show Rate Awareness
I know my approximate show rate right now - and I know whether it is going up, going down, or staying flat compared to last month.
Not a guess. An actual sense of the number and its direction. If you do not know it, the dashboard does.
