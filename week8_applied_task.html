This Week's Task
Identify your Murphy. Use them. See what happens.

Before your next visit to a location where you have pending maybes, identify one existing donor owner who would be a good cheerleader. Someone who's been in the program, whose dog is healthy and happy, and who would be willing to say so to a stranger. Then when you see a maybe and your Murphy in the same lobby — make the introduction. Document what happened. Be honest about whether it moved the needle.

A Patricia Moment
Patricia completed this task.

Patricia identified her Murphy. Patricia did not introduce her Murphy to anyone. Patricia did not think to. Patricia's Murphy was in the lobby at the same time as two of her maybes this week. Patricia gave both maybes her full pitch. Both said they'd think about it. Patricia added them to her email list. Patricia's email list now has 29 people on it. Patricia considers this progress.

Which location are you targeting?

Pick one where you have at least one maybe pending and at least one existing donor who comes in regularly.

Who is your Murphy at this location?

Dog's name, owner's name, or however you know them. What makes them a good cheerleader — how long have they been in the program, what are they like?

Who is your maybe?

The hesitant parent you're hoping to close. What's their specific hesitation — spouse defer, temperament worry, serial next-timer, curious newcomer, or something else?

Which closer are you planning to use?

🌱 Breadcrumb via staff

Combination

After the visit — what happened?

Did you get the Murphy and the maybe in the same place? Did you make the introduction? How did it go? Did it move the needle, and if not, why not? Be honest — a result of "they still said they'd think about it, but I learned something" is a valid and useful answer.
