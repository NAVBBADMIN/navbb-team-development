This Week — Two Things
One conversation to return to. One location to decide about.

Part 1 — The Conversation to Return To

Name one specific conversation from the last 60–90 days. Who was it with, at which location, and what made it feel promising? What do you remember about what you said that seemed to land?

What made it feel promising?

Not just "they seemed interested." What specifically did they respond to? What moment in the conversation felt real?

Your specific reach-out

Not a mass follow-up. One personal message that references something from the original conversation — their dog's name, something they mentioned, a specific reason it connects to them. Write what you're going to say or what you said.

What happened?

Did you hear back? Did they sign up, say not yet, or go quiet again? If you haven't sent it yet, what's your plan and timeline?

Part 2 — The Habit Location

Name one location you're still visiting out of habit that hasn't given you a real signal in months. How long have you been going? What have you tried?
