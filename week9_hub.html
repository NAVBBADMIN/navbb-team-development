The Scoring — Know This Cold
How Points Are Earned
2 pts

Each new dog that completes screening and is approved as an active donor. This is the primary metric.

1 pt

Each approved donor that shows up on a scheduled donation day. Capped at once per dog per month — dogs can only donate once per month.

Eligible donors: dogs 50 lbs or more, ages 1–7, not on chronic medication, negative heartworm test in the last year, current on vaccines and preventatives, never received a blood transfusion. Only eligible, approved donors earn points — no exceptions.
