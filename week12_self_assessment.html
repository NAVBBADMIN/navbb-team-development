A Patricia Moment
Patricia scored herself a 4 on relationship depth.

Patricia has visited Barkability eleven times. Patricia knows Jennifer at the front desk. Patricia considers this a strong relationship. Patricia cannot name a single parent at Barkability. Patricia has never asked Jennifer to text her when a new eligible dog joins. Patricia has never followed up with a specific parent by name after a visit. Patricia waves at Jennifer when she arrives. Jennifer waves back. Patricia calls this a partnership.

Question 1 of 6 · Relationship Depth
At my anchor locations, I can name at least one parent — not a manager, a parent — who would recognize my name if a staff member mentioned it.
Not someone you've talked to once. Someone who would say "oh yes, I know her" if the daycare brought you up.
Question 2 of 6 · Relationship Depth
I have received at least one inbound contact from a parent — a call, a text, a message — that I did not initiate, in the last month.
Someone who reached out to you because they thought of something relevant to the program. Not a response to your outreach.
Question 3 of 6 · Individual Connection
After visits, I follow up with specific parents by name — not mass emails, individual messages about their specific dog or situation.
The follow-up that says "I was thinking about Cooper specifically" rather than "thanks for stopping to chat today."
Question 4 of 6 · Individual Connection
I have made a specific ask of a staff member — such as letting me know when a new large dog joins — that has turned them into someone who works for the program between my visits.
One conversation, one specific ask, that created an ongoing system you didn't have to keep maintaining.
Question 5 of 6 · Territory Management
I have identified — and acted on — at least one location on my schedule that is giving me the polite brush-off and doesn't merit the continued time.
Not just noticed it. Done something about it — redirected the time, made one final targeted attempt, or had a clear plan for what comes next.
Question 6 of 6 · Territory Management
When I'm at a location, I leave with at least one specific name — a parent I talked to — and a plan to follow up with that person specifically.
Not a general sense of how it went. A name, and what you're going to do with it.
