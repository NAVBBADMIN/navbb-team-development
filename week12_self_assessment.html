A Patricia Moment
Patricia scored herself a 4 on relationship depth.

Patricia has been to each of her seventeen locations at least five times. Patricia knows the name of the front desk staff at eleven of them. Patricia gave herself a 4 because she considers this strong relationship knowledge. Patricia cannot name a single parent. Patricia has never been contacted by a parent between visits. Patricia wonders why her referral count is zero. Patricia is adding two more locations next month.

Question 1 of 6 · Relationship Depth
At my anchor locations, I can name at least one parent — not a manager or staff member, a parent — who would recognize my name if the daycare mentioned it.
Not someone I talked to once. Someone who knows who I am and what I do.
Question 2 of 6 · Relationship Depth
In the last month, I have been contacted by a parent who initiated the conversation — not because I followed up first, but because something came up and they thought of me.
A text, a call, a message through the daycare. Something they sent without being prompted.
Question 3 of 6 · Individual Connection
When I visit a location, I spend time in conversation with at least one parent per visit — not pitching, just talking about their dog, their situation, something personal.
The conversation that builds the relationship, not the conversation that closes the sign-up.
Question 4 of 6 · Individual Connection
I follow up with specific parents by name after conversations, not just with locations in general.
"Hey Sarah, following up on what we talked about last Tuesday about Max" — not "following up on my visit to Camp Bow Wow."
Question 5 of 6 · Strategic Territory
I can honestly assess which locations on my schedule are worth continued investment and which ones have been giving me a polite no for months.
Not which ones you hope will come around. Which ones the evidence says are actually open.
Question 6 of 6 · Strategic Territory
I know the quality of each location in my territory — not just the count of locations, but which ones are actively producing relationships vs. which ones are just familiar stops on a route.
The distinction between a location that's in your schedule and a location that's in your program.
