A Patricia Moment
Patricia is doing the applied task right now.

Patricia used the investment frame this week. Patricia told a parent that the program was a great investment opportunity for their dog. The parent asked if it was some kind of financial product. Patricia has since clarified her phrasing. Patricia is now telling parents that the program invests in their dog like a stock portfolio. Patricia's manager has been notified.

Part 1 - The Investment Frame Conversation

Describe one conversation this week where you used the investment frame deliberately - leading with what the program puts into the dog before asking for the commitment. What situation were you in? Was the parent already interested, or were they starting to disengage?

What specifically did you say?

Not a summary - the actual words you used to introduce the investment frame. What did you say about the bloodwork, parasite prevention, health monitoring?

How did they respond?

Did it change the conversation? Did they engage differently than before? Did someone who was walking away stop and turn around? Or did it not land the way you expected?

Part 2 - Your Next Three Sign-Ups

For each of your next three sign-ups this week, document the three things: did they hear the six-donation commitment, did you confirm the collection day works, did they leave with specific dates? This is not about grading yourself - it is about making the checklist a habit.
