Part 1 - One Real Call

Name a specific pending or at-risk donor you called this week. Not emailed. Called. Who was it, when did you call, and did they pick up?

What happened on the call?

If they picked up: what did they say? Did they sound confident or hesitant? What information did the conversation give you that an email could not? If voicemail: did you call back the same day?

What is your read on this donor?

Based on the call, what do you expect to happen at the next event? Confident they are coming, still uncertain, or leaning toward archive?

Part 2 - One Archive Decision

Name one donor you decided to archive this week after two unanswered contacts. Not because you gave up - because you made an informed decision that the name was not serving your pipeline.

Who and why?

Dog name, how many times you attempted contact, over what timeframe, and what specifically made you decide this was the right call.

What does it tell you about your list?

Is this a one-off, or does archiving this name reveal something about a pattern? About a daycare, a time of year, a type of sign-up conversation that is not producing real donors?
