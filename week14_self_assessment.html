A Patricia Moment
Patricia knows her sign-up count. Patricia does not know her show rate.

Patricia has gotten 12 sign-ups this month. Patricia considers this excellent. When asked what her first-screening show rate is, Patricia checks her spreadsheet. Patricia's spreadsheet has a column for "Signed Up." Patricia's spreadsheet does not have a column for "Showed Up to Screening." Patricia has never thought about this before. Patricia is going to add the column right now. Patricia considers this progress.

Question 1 of 6 · In the Moment
When someone signs up, I give them a specific next date before they walk away — not "we'll be in touch," but an actual calendar date for their first screening.
The difference between "we'll reach out" and "we'll be here again on Tuesday the 14th — that's your first screening date."
Question 2 of 6 · In the Moment
I ask new sign-ups to put the first screening date in their phone calendar while I'm still standing with them.
Not "remember to mark your calendar" — actually asking them to open their phone and add it right then.
Question 3 of 6 · Follow-Up Quality
When I follow up with a pending donor, I reference something specific from the conversation — their dog's name, something they said, something they were nervous about.
The difference between "Hi, just following up on your registration" and "Hi Sarah, checking in on Cooper ahead of his screening Tuesday."
Question 4 of 6 · Follow-Up Quality
I follow up with pending donors individually, not in group emails or mass outreach.
One message to one person, written for that person — not one message sent to twelve people at once.
Question 5 of 6 · Pipeline Awareness
I know my approximate first-screening show rate — what percentage of my sign-ups actually make it to their first screening appointment.
Not a guess — a real sense of the number based on what you've seen.
Question 6 of 6 · Pipeline Awareness
When I recruit at a location, I know when the collection team's next visit to that location is — and I use that to give new sign-ups a specific, close date.
Recruiting two weeks before the collection team visits means a 14-day gap instead of a 90-day one.
