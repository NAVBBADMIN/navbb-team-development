Two Recruiters, Same Territory

~8 min · Click through each scene · One question at the end.
Scene 1 of 6
The Setup

Same territory size. Same number of available locations. One recruiter is busier. One recruiter has parents who call her.

Recruiter A has twelve locations on her schedule. She visits each one regularly. She knows the staff at every location. She's consistent and reliable and her managers appreciate her.

Recruiter B has eight locations. She visits each one less frequently than Recruiter A visits hers. She has three parents — real people, by name — who contact her directly when something comes up.

Watch what the next month looks like for each of them.

Recruiter A — A Busy Month

Recruiter A generates activity. Activity doesn't always generate donors.

Recruiter A visits forty-eight times across her twelve locations in a month. She has good conversations at most of them. Staff know her name. Several managers tell her they're happy she comes by. She leaves flyers at every visit. She follows up by email to twelve locations after particularly good conversations.

3
New donor sign-ups from 48 visits across 12 locations. All three were one-time conversations. None of them have produced a referral. Recruiter A doesn't know the names of the owners who signed up.

Recruiter A is not doing anything wrong. She is doing everything right. She just doesn't have anyone working for her when she's not in the building.

Recruiter B — A Quieter Month

Recruiter B generates fewer visits. Her relationships generate the rest.

Recruiter B visits twenty-six times across her eight locations. She spends more time per visit than Recruiter A does. She asks about specific dogs by name. She remembers conversations from three visits ago. She follows up with individual parents, not just locations.

She gets two calls this month she didn't initiate. One from a parent named Maria whose neighbor just adopted a Great Pyrenees. One from a daycare manager whose new hire mentioned she had a golden retriever that was the right size and age. Neither of those conversations required a visit.

The Month's Results

Same territory. Very different infrastructure.

Recruiter A — 12 Locations

48 visits. 3 sign-ups. 0 inbound contacts. 0 referrals. 0 parents who know her name without looking at a flyer. She is working hard. She is producing from her own effort exclusively. When she stops visiting, her results stop with her.

Recruiter B — 8 Locations

26 visits. 5 sign-ups. 2 inbound contacts. 3 referrals in progress. 3 parents who would answer a call from her number without seeing who it is. When she doesn't visit, her relationships continue working.

Recruiter B produced more from fewer visits. Not because she worked differently on the day of the visit. Because she worked differently on every visit that came before.

The Polite Brush-Off

Recruiter A has two locations she's been visiting for four months without a single sign-up.

Location 7 is always welcoming. The manager is friendly. He says it's not a good time every visit but always invites her back. She's been there nine times. She has never gotten past the reception area. She has never spoken to a parent. She adds them to her schedule again next month.

Recruiter B had a location like this. She went four times. On the fourth visit she said directly: "I want to make sure I'm not wasting your time or mine. Is this something you'd want to revisit in a few months, or is it just not the right fit?" The manager appreciated the honesty. He said maybe in the spring. She removed it from her monthly schedule and set a reminder for March.

The time Recruiter B reclaimed from that one honest conversation went to a location where a parent named Maria was already half-sold. Reading the signal early is not giving up. It's protecting the time that belongs to your open doors.

Your Turn

At your locations right now — are you Recruiter A or Recruiter B? And where's your Location 7?

Be specific. How many of your active locations have a parent you know by name who would contact you without being prompted? And name the location that has been giving you the polite brush-off — what's your plan for it?
