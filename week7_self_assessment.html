Question 1 of 6 · Adaptability
When my plan for a location visit falls apart — bad weather, location change, lower turnout than expected — I adjust in the moment rather than going through the motions.
Not pivoting to a different location — adjusting your approach at the location you're already at.
Question 2 of 6 · Adaptability
When a difficult location has stopped producing, my first instinct is to try something different — not to go back the same way and hope for a different result.
Persistence and repetition are not the same thing.
Question 3 of 6 · Logistics Awareness
Before visiting a location for the first time — or a location with a known setup challenge — I find out about the physical logistics before I show up.
Indoor vs. outdoor pickup. Where dogs come out. What the lobby actually looks like. Best day and time. Who the right contact is.
Question 4 of 6 · Logistics Awareness
When I have a location with an outdoor or drive-thru pickup, I've thought specifically about where to position myself and when to be there.
Not just "I show up and try to talk to people" — a specific plan for the physical setup.
Question 5 of 6 · Creative Problem-Solving
When an owner or decision-maker has been hard to reach, I've tried at least two genuinely different approaches — not just variations of the same tactic.
Email vs. email with different subject line is not two different approaches. Email and an unscheduled pop-in with pastries is.
Question 6 of 6 · Creative Problem-Solving
I've asked for something at a location that I wasn't sure they'd say yes to — and gotten it.
System access, an unscheduled visit on a different day, a pop-in with the owner, a favor from staff that wasn't part of the standard arrangement.
