A Patricia Moment
Patricia does not mention the six-donation commitment upfront.

Patricia does not want to scare people off. Patricia has a high sign-up count and a low show rate. Patricia has never connected these two things. Patricia scored herself a 4 on this self-assessment. Patricia's last three sign-ups did not know the team comes on Tuesdays. One of them called on a Wednesday to ask where everyone was. Patricia is designing a new flyer.

Question 1 of 6 - The Investment Frame
When I talk to parents about the program, I lead with what the program invests in their dog - not just what I am asking them to give.
Bloodwork, parasite prevention, health monitoring - these are real benefits the dog receives. Do you make them concrete before you make the ask?
Question 2 of 6 - The Investment Frame
When a parent seems to be disengaging or ready to walk away, I know how to change the frame of the conversation instead of just adding more information.
More information does not work on someone who has already decided to leave. Changing what the conversation is about is different from adding more of what was not working.
Question 3 of 6 - The Commitment
I tell parents about the six-donation minimum commitment before they sign up - out loud, clearly, as part of the conversation.
Not in the paperwork. Not implied. Said directly: "We ask for a minimum of six donations over twelve months." Then confirmed: "Does that work for you?"
Question 4 of 6 - The Commitment
Before a parent signs up, I confirm that the collection day actually works for their schedule.
Not assumed. Asked directly: "Our team comes every Tuesday - does Tuesday work for you?" If it does not, that conversation happens now, not after they register and never show up.
Question 5 of 6 - The Dates
After sign-up, I make sure parents have the specific next donation date and at least the following quarter of dates in a format they can actually use.
Texted, emailed, written down, on their calendar. Not "check the website." A specific date they can point to.
Question 6 of 6 - The Overall Ask
When a parent leaves a conversation with me, they know: what the program invests in their dog, that the commitment is six donations over twelve months, and what day the team comes.
All three. Not one or two. All three, every time.
